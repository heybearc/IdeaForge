## Core concept

An adult talks to one companion that has a face and a live voice. The use is everyday stress, loneliness, and someone to talk to. It is a wellness companion. It is not therapy and it is not emergency care.

Someone brought a ChatGPT business plan (`~/Downloads/Document.docx`, 2026-10-31) and asked to partner or to pay for the build. The plan’s first version was a no-code text chatbot plus journaling, with voice as a later paid feature. The product to test is the face and the voice.

## Decision

Build a local spike and see if it is worth continuing. Do not form the company, do not take public users, and do not stand up blue-green for the spike.

## What the spike has to show

- One adult, one companion
- Live voice
- A face that moves with the voice
- A visible “this is AI, not care” line
- A crisis path, separate from the personality prompt, that shows 988 and 911

## Held back on purpose

Stripe, the $14.99 tier, mood and journal tools, extra personalities, phone apps, and containers. The plan’s $3,500 / 90-day budget is a sketch. Legal review and a clinician’s look at the crisis path come before any stranger uses it.

## Already shipping a face and a voice

Kindroid and Replika. Sesame is live voice without a face the user designs. Wysa, Youper, and Character.AI were named in the source plan and are the wrong comparison for this slice.

## Next step

None. This idea is closed.
