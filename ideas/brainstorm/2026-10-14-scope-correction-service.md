# Scope Correction Service

**Date Created:** 2026-10-14  
**Category:** automation-services  
**Status:** brainstorm  
**Creator:** Cory

A client describes a web app. An AI pass looks up who already does it and names the weak assumption. Cory rewrites the plan and builds only the milestone that survived. The client pays for that milestone.

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

Sell the correction, then the build. The draft scope is what the client almost bought. The paid work is the revised first milestone, shipped with Cursor.

### Problem Being Solved

AI scoping tools write a plan that sounds finished. The client pays a builder and gets the wrong app. The missing step is someone who will cut the plan before the build starts.

### Target Audience

A founder or small business about to hire a web developer. They pay for a milestone, not a subscription.

### Unique Value Proposition

ScopeDesk, Afren, Startive, and Venturee generate the scope or the score. This sells the cut, and then ships the piece that remains.

## ⚠️ Risk Assessment

### Top 3 Risks

1. **The client wanted the original scope**: They pay a builder who agrees with them, and the correction loses the job.
   - **Likelihood**: High
   - **Impact**: High
   - **Mitigation**: Offer the revised milestone as the price. If they insist on the rejected build, decline.

2. **The generators are good enough**: ScopeDesk or Afren plus any freelancer covers the same ground.
   - **Likelihood**: Medium
   - **Impact**: High
   - **Mitigation**: The test is a client who pays for the cut, not a client who pays for a document.

3. **Hours fill up on one build**: There is no product left when the engagement ends.
   - **Likelihood**: High
   - **Impact**: Medium
   - **Mitigation**: Keep the intake transcript. A tool only starts after a second client asks for the same front door.
